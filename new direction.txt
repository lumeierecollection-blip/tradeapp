14. DYNAMIC TAKE-PROFIT
============================================================

THIS IS CRITICAL.

REMOVE THE CURRENT CONCEPT OF A FIXED TARGET MULTIPLIER IF THAT IS WHAT PRODUCES THE CURRENT 2.2% / 4.4% BEHAVIOUR.

Do NOT simply replace it with:

risk × 5

or:

risk × 10

That would be fake precision.

Instead calculate target candidates from real data.

Potential target candidates:

1. volatility-based projection
2. market-structure level
3. recent swing extension
4. momentum/continuation potential

Then evaluate the candidates.

For LONG:

Target should generally be below a meaningful opposing resistance level when appropriate.

For SHORT:

Target should generally be above a meaningful opposing support level when appropriate.

Target must be:

- achievable relative to current volatility
- structurally sensible
- compatible with the setup
- compatible with risk/reward

If the market does not support a large move:

DO NOT force a large target.

This means the system may legitimately produce:

while another asset may produce:

SOL target = 8.7%

and another may produce:

ETH target = 5.1%.

That is expected.

17. FOUR SETUP TIERS
============================================================

Implement four setup classifications.

The percentages MUST NOT define the tiers.

The quality of the market setup defines the tier.

--------------------------------------------
WEAK SETUP
--------------------------------------------

Some evidence exists.

But confirmation is limited.

Possible characteristics:

- weak momentum
- mixed timeframe confirmation
- limited volume confirmation
- weaker structure
- mediocre risk/reward

This is a low-conviction opportunity.

--------------------------------------------
NORMAL SETUP
--------------------------------------------

Multiple indicators agree.

Possible characteristics:

- reasonable momentum
- reasonable volume
- supportive RSI
- some multi-timeframe agreement
- acceptable structure
- acceptable risk/reward

--------------------------------------------
STRONG SETUP
--------------------------------------------

Strong confluence.

Possible characteristics:

- multi-timeframe agreement
- strong momentum
- strong relative volume
- supportive RSI
- strong market structure
- favourable volatility
- good risk/reward
- meaningful room toward target

--------------------------------------------
VERY STRONG MOMENTUM
--------------------------------------------

This tier MUST be rare.

Require exceptional alignment.

Possible requirements:

- strong momentum across multiple timeframes
- strong volume expansion
- trend agreement
- strong structural confirmation
- breakout/continuation confirmation
- favourable volatility
- acceptable RSI condition
- strong risk/reward
- sufficient liquidity
- no major contradictory signal

Do not classify a signal as Very Strong merely because its target is large.

A large target does not equal a strong setup.

18. CONFIDENCE VS EXPECTED MOVE
============================================================

These MUST remain separate.

Confidence means:

"How strongly do the available signals agree?"

Expected move means:

"How much movement does the current market structure/volatility reasonably support?"

Example:

Expected move: 4.8%

can be valid.

Do NOT interpret 91% as a guaranteed 91% chance of profit.

Use terminology:

confidence
setup quality
expected move
risk/reward
supporting evidence

Avoid:

guaranteed
certain
risk-free
sure profit
